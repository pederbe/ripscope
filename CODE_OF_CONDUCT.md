# Code of conduct

Ripscope follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus criticism
on ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, personal attacks and
discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or
any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer at https://pederbe.dev/#contact

Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or restrict
participation as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
